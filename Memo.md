# Shopee earns more per order but keeps little of it, and FY2026's US$1 billion depends on the mega-sale season

*Based on Sea Limited's quarterly earnings releases, Q1 2019 to Q2 2026 (SEC Form 6-K). Model: `Shopee_Monee_Unit_Economics.xlsx`.*

## What I found

**1. Shopee now takes twice as much of each sale in fees and ads.** Core marketplace revenue (transaction fees and advertising) rose from 5.2% of GMV in Q3 2022 to 11.2% in Q2 2026. Logistics revenue moved the other way, from a peak of 4.0% of GMV in Q1 2023 to 1.8%, because Sea nets more shipping subsidies against it. Average order value has stayed at about US$9–10 since 2021.

**2. Growth is coming more from the take rate.** Marketplace revenue grew 48.5% year-on-year in Q2 2026. About 61% of that came from more orders and 37% from a higher take rate. A year earlier the take rate explained only 11%.

**3. The extra revenue is not reaching the bottom line.** Shopee's adjusted EBITDA was US$0.06 per order in Q2 2026, or 0.67% of GMV. That is below its first profitable stretch (1.2% of GMV in Q1 2023) and has not risen while the take rate kept climbing. The gains are being reinvested: shipping subsidies are netted against logistics revenue, and Shopee's sales and marketing expenses rose 56.6% year-on-year in Q2 2026, faster than marketplace revenue (48.9%). Shopee also fell back into losses from Q3 2023 to Q2 2024 (−US$346 million in Q3 2023) when Sea chose to reinvest, so its profit can be switched off quickly.

**4. Nowcast: US$1 billion in FY2026 is possible, but it needs an H2 unlike 2025's.** *Pre-results estimate (nowcast) made on 15 Oct 2026 from H1 2026 data, before Sea reports Q3 2026 (mid-November 2026) and FY2026 (around March 2027).* H1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was US$478.6 million, so H2 must deliver US$521.4 million. At H1's growth (29.5%) and margin (0.63% of GMV), Shopee reaches about US$1,043 million. If H2 margin falls back to H2 2025's 0.56% and growth slows by 5 points, it reaches about US$962 million. The break-even H2 margin is about 0.58% of GMV, only slightly above H2 2025's level. In 2025, H2 EBITDA was 79% of H1. If that seasonal pattern repeats, FY2026 lands near US$856 million.

**5. Monee is growing fast, and its headline credit ratio flatters it.** Loans (incl. off-book) grew from US$3.1 billion (Q4 2023) to US$11.1 billion (Q2 2026). Over the same period the adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 31% to 21%. The reported 90-day NPL ratio improved to 1.0%. But when today's bad loans are divided by the smaller loan book of a year earlier, the ratio sits around 1.6–2.0% and has not improved. Fast growth dilutes the ratio, which is why it looks better.

## What to watch

- **Q3 2026 results (mid-November).** The base case implies Q3 adjusted EBITDA of roughly US$264 million (Q3 GMV of about US$41.7 billion at a 0.63% margin). A Q3 margin near 0.56% would point to the bear case.
- **Take rate vs margin.** If the core take rate keeps rising while EBITDA per order stays at US$0.05–0.06, Shopee is buying growth with its higher fees. That is a choice, not a problem, as long as order growth holds near 27–30%.
- **Monee seasoning.** Watch whether the NPL ratio rises as the 2025–2026 loan cohorts age.

## Limits

- Sea prints most figures rounded (GMV to US$0.1 billion), so ratios carry about ±1% relative error. Adjusted EBITDA comes from exact tables.
- Sea did not report GMV or orders for Q1–Q2 2023. These are derived from growth rates reported a year later and match the FY2023 totals within 0.7%.
- Monee changed its loan measure twice (2023Q3, 2023Q4). Its pre-2023Q3 NPL figures are bounds, and the Q4 2022 drop came mainly from a shorter write-off period.
- There is no country split, so this is Shopee overall, not Shopee Indonesia. The FY2026 scenarios are a simple driver-based nowcast of results Sea has not yet reported, not Sea's guidance. They will be checked against the Q3 2026 and FY2026 releases.
