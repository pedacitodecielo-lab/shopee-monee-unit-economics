# Shopee earns more per order but keeps little of it, and FY2026's US$1 billion depends on a stronger second half

*Based on Sea Limited's quarterly earnings releases, Q1 2019 to Q2 2026 (SEC Form 6-K). Model: `Shopee_Monee_Unit_Economics.xlsx`.*

## What I found

**1. Shopee's take from fees and ads has roughly doubled.** Core marketplace revenue (transaction fees and advertising) was about 5% of GMV in Q3 2022. It rose to 11.1% in Q2 2026. Logistics (value-added services) revenue moved the other way, from about 4% of GMV in late 2022 to early 2023 down to 1.8%. Sea attributes recent declines in this line to "higher revenue net-off against shipping subsidies". Average order value has stayed at roughly US$9–11 since late 2021.

**2. More of the growth now comes from the take rate.** Marketplace revenue grew 48.9% year-on-year in Q2 2026. About 61% of that growth came from more orders and 37% from a higher take rate. In Q2 2025, the take rate explained about 15%.

**3. The extra revenue is not reaching the bottom line.** Shopee's adjusted EBITDA was about US$0.06 per order in Q2 2026, or 0.67% of GMV. That is below its first profitable quarters (about 1.1–1.2% of GMV in Q4 2022 and Q1 2023). Sales and marketing expenses rose 56.6% year-on-year in Q2 2026, faster than marketplace revenue (48.9%). Shopee also fell back into losses from Q3 2023 to Q2 2024 (−US$346 million in Q3 2023). At the time, Sea said it would "prioritize investing in the business to increase our market share".

**4. Nowcast: US$1 billion in FY2026 is possible, but it needs a second half stronger than 2025's.** *This is a pre-results estimate made on 15 Oct 2026 from H1 2026 data, before Sea reports Q3 2026 (mid-November 2026) and FY2026 (around March 2027).* In the Q2 2026 release, Sea's Chairman and CEO Forrest Li said: "we are optimistic that Shopee will achieve the milestone of US$1 billion in adjusted EBITDA for the full year". The arithmetic:
- H1 2026 adjusted EBITDA was US$478.6 million, so H2 must deliver US$521.4 million.
- At H1's GMV growth (29.5%) and margin (0.63% of GMV), Shopee reaches about US$1,043 million.
- If the H2 margin falls back to H2 2025's 0.56% and growth is 5 points slower, it reaches about US$962 million.
- With growth 3 points faster and Q2 2026's margin (0.67%), it reaches about US$1,087 million.
- The break-even H2 margin is about 0.58% of GMV.
- In 2025, H2 EBITDA was 79% of H1. If H2 2026 follows the same pattern, FY2026 lands near US$856 million.

**5. Monee is growing fast, and fast growth flatters its headline NPL ratio.**
- Loans (including off-book) grew from US$3.1 billion in Q4 2023 to US$11.1 billion in Q2 2026. Over the same period, the adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 31% to 21%.
- The reported 90-day NPL ratio fell from 1.6% to 1.0%.
- I also divided each quarter's NPL amount by the smaller loan book of a year earlier, a lagged view that removes the dilution from new loans that have not had time to go bad. On that view, the ratio held around 1.9–2.0% from Q4 2024 to Q1 2026 and eased to 1.6% in Q2 2026. Credit quality looks stable, but much of the drop in the headline ratio comes from growth rather than better loans.

## What to watch

- **Q3 2026 results (mid-November).** The base case implies Q3 adjusted EBITDA of roughly US$264 million (Q3 GMV of about US$41.7 billion at a 0.63% margin). A Q3 margin near 0.56% would point to the bear case.
- **Take rate vs margin.** If the core take rate keeps rising while EBITDA per order stays around US$0.05–0.06, the higher fees are being spent on growth.
- **Monee seasoning.** Watch whether the lagged NPL view rises again as the 2025–2026 loan cohorts age.

## Limits

- **Rounding and exact figures.** Sea prints the GMV and order bullets rounded (GMV to US$0.1 billion). Marketplace and Monee revenue are taken from Sea's exact segment tables from Q1 2024 onward. Earlier quarters use the rounded bullets, so the 2022–2023 take rates are approximate. Adjusted EBITDA always comes from exact tables.
- **Q1–Q2 2023 gap.** Sea did not report GMV or orders for Q1–Q2 2023. These quarters are derived from the growth rates Sea reported a year later, and they match the FY2023 totals within 0.7%.
- **Monee's measures changed.** Monee changed its loan measure twice (2023Q3 and 2023Q4). Its NPL figures before 2023Q3 are only bounds. The Q4 2022 drop came mainly from a shorter write-off period, as Sea disclosed. The lagged NPL view uses NPL percentages printed to one decimal, so it is approximate.
- **Scope of the nowcast.** There is no country split, so this covers Shopee overall, not Shopee Indonesia. The FY2026 scenarios are a simple driver-based nowcast of results Sea has not yet reported. They are not Sea's guidance.
